Jewel POS

Stock and billing software for jewellery shops in Pakistan. Runs on your own Windows PC and works without the internet.

Download for Windows

What it does

  • Stock by weight and purity: tagged pieces and gold sold by the gram
  • Post the day's gold rate once, and every price follows it
  • Sales bills, returns, old gold taken at the counter and bought over it
  • Udhaar (credit), bayana (advance) and karigar (goldsmith) jobs
  • Expenses, the closing cash count, reports and zakat
  • English and Urdu

Your data stays with you

Everything the shop records is kept on the shop's own PC. There is no account to make and no server of ours that your data goes to. Backups are taken automatically on the PC. If you choose, a copy also goes to your own Google Drive every evening, so the shop's records survive a stolen or broken computer. Read exactly what that means in the privacy policy.
